# ContractPro AI
## Getting Started Guide

---

**Welcome!** ContractPro AI helps you capture more leads, create professional quotes in seconds, and never miss a customer call or text. This guide walks you through everything your new system can do.

---

# Part 1: Your Smart CRM & AI Quoting

## Dashboard

Your home base. See at a glance:
- New leads waiting for follow-up
- Active jobs in progress
- Recent revenue

## Lead Capture

Customers fill out your personalized quote request form online. They can:
- Describe their project
- Upload photos and videos
- Provide contact info

Leads automatically appear in your dashboard — no manual entry needed.

## AI Quote Generator

Create professional quotes in seconds:

1. **Describe the job** — Type what the customer needs in plain English  
   *Example: "Install 200 sq ft flagstone patio with gravel base"*

2. **AI does the math** — Automatically calculates:
   - Materials needed
   - Labor hours
   - Real prices from Home Depot & Lowe's

3. **Pick your price tier** — Get 3 quote options:
   - **Budget** — Lower markup for price-sensitive customers
   - **Standard** — Your typical pricing
   - **Premium** — Higher quality materials, higher margin

## Send Quotes to Customers

- Click "Send to Client" — opens your email with the quote link ready
- Customer views a professional, branded quote online
- Customer can **sign electronically** to accept

## Client Management

Keep all your customers organized:
- Contact info (phone, email, address)
- Project history
- Notes and communications

## Invoice Creation

When a quote is accepted:
- Convert it to an invoice with one click
- Send to customer for payment

# Part 2: Your AI-Powered Phone Assistant

## Your Business Phone Number

You get a dedicated phone number for your business. Customers call or text this number, and the AI handles the rest.

## Never Miss a Call

When you can't answer:
- AI sends the customer a text automatically
- Includes your quote request link so they can submit project details
- You get a text notification about the missed call

When you do answer:
- Call is recorded and transcribed
- AI summarizes the conversation
- Summary is texted to you for your records

## Smart Text Responses

When a new customer texts you:
- AI reads their message
- Sends a friendly auto-reply
- Includes your quote request link

*Example auto-reply:*  
> "Hi, thanks for reaching out! Can you give us a bit more detail about the issue? [Your Quote Request Link]"

## Call Follow-Ups

After every customer call:
- AI texts the customer with a follow-up
- Links them to submit photos/videos of the project
- You're notified immediately

## Two-Way Texting

Reply to customers directly:
- Text back from your phone
- Messages go through your business number
- Keeps your personal number private
